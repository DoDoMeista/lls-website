Who We Are

Built for
the North

Lundqvist Lightside has been part of the Nordic esports landscape since 2014. Based in Northern Sweden, we develop competitive talent across League of Legends, Valorant and CS2 — representing Sweden at the NLC and competing in the Valve ecosystem with our CS2 squad.
